# ADR-0026: Delivery horizon: MVP in 8 weeks, full v1 by ~week 26

- **Status:** Accepted (locked decision #34 in MASTER_SPEC §1.4)
- **Date:** 2026-09-25
- **Deciders:** Shakil Javed (owner)
- **Spec references:** §14

## Context
The owner wants early revenue and then a full platform.

## Decision
MVP (P00–P05) covers core CRM, leads, pipeline, basic reports and billing, in production in eu-central-1 by week 8 against the §14.1 gate. Full v1 (P06–P12) follows by ~week 26 against the §14.2 gate. Scope protection: if a phase slips, feature breadth is cut before quality gates (per the §1.5 priority order), after asking the owner.

## Consequences
+ A clear gate-driven plan.
− The schedule is aggressive relative to scope (see the risks).

## Alternatives rejected
A longer MVP (delays learning).

> Changing this decision requires the owner's approval (§0.3) and a new superseding ADR.
